# Tung Tung Tung Sahur: research brief for game story text

Confidence: **[F]** = sourced fact, **[L]** = fan lore / widespread trope, **[?]** = unverified. Several search results were SEO content farms; I ignored claims that only they made.

## 1. Origin (facts)
- **Creator/date [F]:** Indonesian TikToker @noxaasht ("Noxa"; Wikipedia names Fernanda Bagas Indrastata, image made with DALL-E). Earliest known post: **28 Feb 2025**, an AI image of a wooden stick-man holding a baseball bat. [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/tung-tung-tung-sahur), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tung_Tung_Tung_Sahur), [meme.com](https://meme.com/memes/tung-tung-tung-sahur) (claims 42.5M views).
- **Voice line [F]:** Indonesian text-to-speech narration. Paraphrase: a frightening *anomaly* that appears only during sahur; if you are called for sahur three times and do not answer, it comes to your house. [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/tung-tung-tung-sahur), [GNFI](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2025/04/23/anomali-tung-tung-sahur-ekspresi-kebudayaan-indonesia-di-kancah-dunia)
- **Look [F]:** anthropomorphic wooden log / kentongan with arms and legs, carrying a baseball bat. The bat is read as a *pentungan*, the stick used to strike the kentongan. Big round eyes are from general knowledge, not confirmed in sources. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tung_Tung_Tung_Sahur), [GNFI](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2025/04/23/anomali-tung-tung-sahur-ekspresi-kebudayaan-indonesia-di-kancah-dunia)
- **Name [F]:** "tung tung tung sahur" is an existing Indonesian wake-up call; "tung" imitates the drum (also "rumbling" in Sundanese per Wikipedia). KYM cites a 2013 tweet as an early use of the phrase.
- **Format [F]:** uses the Italian-brainrot template (TTS voice presenting an AI "anomaly"); KYM suggests French Bitcoin Burger IbraTV videos as a possible influence.
- **Early spread [F]:** 10 Mar 2025 bat-swing-then-explode video (5.3M views in a week); 22 Mar hand-drawn tutorial (3.7M); a joke death notice ("returned to his planet"); powerscaling vs Brr Brr Patapim. [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/tung-tung-tung-sahur)

## 2. The real tradition (facts)
- **Sahur** is the pre-dawn meal before the Ramadan fast. Neighbourhood groups walk the lanes shouting "Sahur! Sahur!", often beating **kentongan** (hollow bamboo/wood slit drums), mosque **bedug** drums, even pot lids. Regional names exist, e.g. *rontek* in Pacitan (from "ronda" + "thektek", the bamboo sound). [Jawa Pos](https://www.jawapos.com/islami/2602070176/9-tradisi-sahur-yang-masih-bertahan-di-kampung-sampai-sekarang), [Marinews: Rontek](https://marinews.mahkamahagung.go.id/serba-serbi/rontek-tradisi-bangunkan-sahur-masyarakat-pacitan-0ZJ)
- **Why the noise:** before loudspeakers reached whole villages, people made enough racket to wake everyone; kentongan also signalled danger or gathered villagers. [GNFI](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2025/04/23/anomali-tung-tung-sahur-ekspresi-kebudayaan-indonesia-di-kancah-dunia), [Marinews](https://marinews.mahkamahagung.go.id/serba-serbi/rontek-tradisi-bangunkan-sahur-masyarakat-pacitan-0ZJ)
- **Timing [F]:** calls typically start ~1.5 h before imsak and repeat about every 30 min; some places start 3-4 h early (criticised as too early). **I found no source for a standard "2-3 AM"; treat it as plausible colour, not fact.** Imsak (stop eating) comes shortly before the dawn (fajr) prayer. [Antara](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/4027119/berkeliling-membangunkan-sahur-dengan-pengeras-suara-perlukah)
- **History/scholars [F]:** Bilal bin Rabah is traditionally the first to wake people for sahur, by adhan. PBNU's LBM says there is no explicit textual mandate but a long tradition, to be done wisely and without disturbing neighbours' rest. [Antara](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/4027119/berkeliling-membangunkan-sahur-dengan-pengeras-suara-perlukah), [Metro TV](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/NrWCoj2n-sejarah-al-musaharati-tradisi-umat-muslim-membangunkan-sahur-saat-ramadan)
- **Modern state [F]:** fading as young people feel awkward or tired; some patrols now use firecrackers (a complaint). Kentongan use is declining because of phones and loudspeakers. [GNFI](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2025/04/23/anomali-tung-tung-sahur-ekspresi-kebudayaan-indonesia-di-kancah-dunia), [Mojok](https://mojok.co/terminal/?p=710)
- **Sensory palette (general knowledge, not sourced):** dark lanes, hollow wooden knocks, shouted calls, lights flicking on in houses, warm rice and dishes, the rush before imsak, the adhan after.

## 3. Evolution
- **Horror framing [F/L]:** the original anomaly/three-calls premise is a tongue-in-cheek horror story; Wikipedia summarises it as punishing anyone who refuses to wake for suhur. [Wikipedia: Muslim meme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim_meme)
- **Seasonal farewell arc [F, widely known]:** near the end of Ramadan 2025, videos showed him leaving, boarding flights, "going home" with the job done (one goodbye tribute passed 1.5M views). [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/tung-tung-tung-sahur)
- **"Triple T" [F]:** shorthand name, and a separate meme: a 15 Apr 2025 3D clip of him replacing a baseball batter, used as a "send in the closer / pinch hitter" reaction GIF (resurged late 2025). Also entries for "Tung Tung God", a dance, a rap song. [KYM: Triple T](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/tung-tung-tung-sahur-triple-t-baseball-gif)
- **Italian brainrot universe [F]:** Tralalero Tralala (three-legged sneaker shark, generally cited as first), Bombardiro Crocodilo (criticised for a Gaza reference), Ballerina Cappuccina, Chimpanzini Bananini, Lirili Larila, Trippi Troppi; Brr Brr Patapim rivalry. Indonesian siblings: Hotspot Bro, Boneca Ambalabu. [Wikipedia: Italian brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_brainrot)
- **Cross-universe gossip [F, joke]:** Ballerina Cappuccina allegedly cheated on Cappuccino Assassino with Sahur.
- **Games/mainstream [F]:** Roblox *Steal a Brainrot*; a Fortnite skin (Wikipedia says April 2026); Viktor Orban posted a dancing 3D Sahur; an ownership lawsuit (Mementum Lab vs Do Big Studios). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tung_Tung_Tung_Sahur)
- **Chant [F]:** the repeated, emphatic "tung tung tung..." (creators vary the count; viewers enjoy it). GNFI notes the satisfying emphatic chanting.
- **Wholesome/protective lore and "sad backstory" edits [?]:** I could not find a documented source, only content-farm claims of a "kind, brave protector". Treat as an unverified fan tendency. Fine to invent in-game, but do not present it as established lore.

## 4. Instantly recognisable tropes
- Three calls, then he shows up (the core rule) [F].
- Wooden log body, bat held upright, big round eyes, standing in a dark doorway or street [F/L].
- Rhythmic hollow wooden "tung" knocks, repeated, count escalating [F/L].
- Bat tapping or dragging on pavement [L, genre trope, not sourced].
- "Anomaly" wording and deadpan TTS tone [F].
- Pinch-hitter entrance (Triple T), exploding/powerscale gags, farewell "going home" at the end of Ramadan [F].
- **Widely known:** three-calls rule, bat + log look, the chant, Italian-brainrot membership, Steal a Brainrot. **Less known:** Triple T baseball GIF, Orban post, lawsuit.

## 5. Sensitivities
- Ramadan and sahur are devotional; the fast is the point. Do not make fasting, prayer, adhan, Quran or God the punchline, and do not mock people who fast or who miss sahur.
- The original is a playful riff by an Indonesian creator. I found no MUI condemnation (searched; absence is not proof). Wikipedia notes Muslim memes are generally accepted as a light way to remind of religious duties.
- Keep the threat mild and comedic. Avoid framing Sahur as punishing faith or as a monster that truly harms. A companion who wakes you in time and looks after the neighbourhood fits the real tradition's community spirit.
- Respect the real etiquette debate: the tradition is already contested for noise. Do not glamorise firecrackers or deliberate disturbance.
- Do not use the adhan, bedug rhythms for prayer, or religious phrases as game SFX or jokes; stick to "sahur!" calls and kentongan.
- Credit the Indonesian roots (creator, kentongan) rather than presenting it as generic "Italian" brainrot; avoid caricature, and avoid Bombardiro's Gaza-adjacent material.
- Legal: character ownership is disputed, so use original art and wording.
