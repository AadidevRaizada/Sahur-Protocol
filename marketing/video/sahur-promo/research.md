# Sahur Protocol promo ("WAKE UP"): research

Researched 13 Oct 2026 for the storyboard and character-design agents. Read with `BRIEF.md` (3D toy look, R3F in Remotion, 75 s, POV, synthesised audio).

## 0. Headline finding: the internet already wrote our ending

- **"We did it Triple T, we popped the AI bubble… Triple T?"** is a May–June 2026 meme. The player and Tung Tung Tung Sahur (fan nickname **Triple T**) pop the AI bubble, then the player turns and he's gone. Because he was AI-made, he dies with it. Fans treat it as a bittersweet goodbye. A 14 May 2026 hand-drawn tribute (art by MAL/@malmalliee, animation by JustBlex) trended on X ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pczF_7qVgEo), [Original vs Meme](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=35KUy_5-vwg), [X post](https://x.com/bebzebk/status/2064752942901514631), [X trending](https://x.com/i/trending/2055328456498721166), [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@mrvmax5656/video/7640544755763514646)). These are user posts, so the numbers are unverified.
  - → use in film: our beats 9–10 *are* this meme, and recognising it is the hook. Nod to the format, but keep our art, audio and staging clearly our own.
- In Jan–Mar 2026 an **"AI-free Tung Tung Tung Sahur art"** trend had artists redrawing him by hand ([KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/ai-free-tung-tung-tung-sahur-art)). Fans say, roughly, that AI made him but people gave him a soul.
  - → use in film: the theme is **a meme born from AI saving you from AI slop, because people loved it into something real.** His warmth should feel hand-made against the glossy Bubble.

## 1. Tung Tung Tung Sahur, the meme

- **Origin.** Created by Indonesian TikToker **Fernanda Bagas Indrastata ("Noxa", @noxaasht)** with DALL-E and posted **28 Feb 2025**, the day before Ramadan began in Indonesia. He prompted for a *pentungan sahur* with a face and legs ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tung_Tung_Tung_Sahur)). The phrase predates the meme: a 2013 tweet reads "Sahur sahur tung tung tung" ([KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/tung-tung-tung-sahur)). The first image shows a tall wooden figure with a bat at a **night bus or train stop**, with big staring eyes and bare feet ([Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/memes/tung-tung-tung-sahur-meme-explained/)).
  - → use in film: the bus stop at night is the canonical image. Show it in beat 1's reel and restage it once in the montage.
- **Sahur and the tradition.** *Sahur* is the pre-dawn meal before the Ramadan fast. In Indonesia, **patrol sahur** groups walk the streets before dawn beating kentongan, drums or recycled objects and chanting, so that nobody sleeps through ([Culture of Jember](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_Jember), [GNFI](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2025/02/26/musik-patrol-musik-kentongan-yang-merupakan-warisan-budaya-asli-jember)). The **kentongan** is a hollow wooden or bamboo slit drum. It was also the night-watch signal that told sleepers they were guarded. In Central Java, the "Doro Muluk" pattern (slow, faster, slow) meant all is safe ([Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/life/2019/12/22/rhythmic-beats-of-kentongan-live-in-digital-era)). Arab and Turkish drummers (*mesaharati*, *davulcu*) do the same thing, sometimes calling people by name until they answer ([Doha News](https://dohanews.co/the-msaharati-a-ramadan-tradition-buried-in-time/)).
  - → use in film: his knock is **the sound of being looked after**, not a threat.
- **Look.** He is an anthropomorphic kentongan: a tall wooden cylinder that is both head and body, wide eyes, big bare feet, thin limbs, and a bat standing in for the *pentungan* beater ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tung_Tung_Tung_Sahur)). He's warm mid-brown wood, which matches the brief's `#b0743a`.
  - → use in film: keep him "too tall, too thin" so a low POV makes him loom. A carved slit can nod to the drum.
- **Audio.** The original voice-over says **"tung" nine times**, then "sahur" ([KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/tung-tung-tung-sahur)). Street chants come in threes, and the "sahuuur" call is long and falling.
  - → use in film: **3** knocks at the door, **2** wind-up TUNGs before the pop, **1** at the end.
- **Lore and universe.** If you're called three times for sahur and don't answer, he comes to your house ([KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/tung-tung-tung-sahur)). His family (context only) is Italian brainrot: Tralalero Tralala, Bombardiro Crocodilo, Ballerina Cappuccina, and his rival Brr Brr Patapim ([PureWow](https://www.purewow.com/family/italian-brainrot)).
- **Emotional range.** Fans have made him scary (the voice-over, horror games), sad (end-of-Ramadan farewell edits of him walking away or "returning to his planet", one with 1.5M views), wholesome (keeping someone company at sahur), and loud (phonk remixes, powerscaling battles) ([Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/memes/tung-tung-tung-sahur-meme-explained/), [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/tung-tung-tung-sahur)).
  - → use in film: the arc goes **horror (door) → protector (hand) → farewell (white)**. Our Sahur is terse and weary, never a jump scare.

## 2. The AI bubble, 2025–2026

- **The discourse.** In Aug 2025 Altman said he believed an AI bubble existed. Nvidia's $100B investment in OpenAI, Oracle's $300B deal and AMD's deal became the **circular deals** story. AI stocks made up about 80% of US gains in 2025 ([Wikipedia: AI bubble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_bubble)). Bloomberg's circular-deals network graphic (Oct 2025) went viral ([FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/2025/10/13/circular-deals-among-ai-companies/)), and commentators called it a "financial ouroboros" ([Asia Times](https://asiatimes.com/2025/10/time-to-worry-about-ais-circular-deals/)).
- **The words.** *Brain rot* was Oxford's 2024 word of the year ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/tech-startup/news/brain-rot-oxfords-2024-word-the-year-what-does-it-even-mean-3766761)). *Slop* was Merriam-Webster's 2025 word, meaning low-quality AI content made in quantity ([ABC7](https://abc7news.com/amp/post/merriam-websters-2025-word-year-is-slop/18288780/)). ChatGPT-favoured words like "delve" have spread into human speech ([The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/chatgpt-is-changing-the-way-we-write-heres-how-and-why-its-a-problem-239601)). LinkedIn hype-speak has a long parody history ([PR Daily](https://www.prdaily.com/linkedin-viral-post-generator-goes-viral-doordash-delivers-new-campaign-and-twitter-gets-into-podcasts)), and so does the **AI Hype Cycle** meme ([meme.com](https://meme.com/memes/ai-hype-cycle-meme)).
- **Slop feeds.** Facebook's "Shrimp Jesus" bait made dead-internet theory feel real ([Interesting Engineering](https://interestingengineering.com/culture/zombie-internet-shrimp-jesus-take-over-social-media)). The Sora app (Oct 2025) is an endless feed made entirely of AI video ([San](https://san.com/cc/openai-launches-sora-2-a-tiktok-style-app-powered-by-ai-video/)).
- **"Almost right" is what makes it creepy.** A mismatch between eyes and face reads as eerie ([MacDorman et al.](https://scholarworks.indianapolis.iu.edu/items/e8fb6458-ddad-4067-b877-1207d720ba0c)). Slop tells include wrong finger counts, garbled almost-text and over-smooth gloss ([Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/how-to-spot-ai-generated-images-3434166)).

**→ Visual cues that read as "the AI bubble" at a glance (no logos):**
1. An iridescent soap bubble with thin-film colour bands, too perfect and too glossy.
2. Hype phrases orbiting it, some nearly-but-not-quite words ("thriled to anounce").
3. A loop of faceless nodes passing **one glowing coin** around: the circular deals as an ouroboros.
4. A line that only goes up: "+∞%".
5. The almost-right face: eyes of different sizes drifting apart, and a smile that doesn't move with them.
6. Every screen in the city shows the same post, with like-counters ticking in sync.
7. A hand with one finger too many.

**→ Sound cues:** notification pings on a too-regular grid, a wineglass-rim whine for the bubble's skin, an **endless riser (Shepard tone)** as it grows, synthetic applause. No voice.

## 4. First-person POV techniques (2.5D and R3F)

- **Eyelid wipe.** Use soft curved lids. Real blinks close fast and open slower ([CMU](https://ri.cmu.edu/?p=121999)). On waking, blur is strongest as the eyes open and then clears ([blink tool](https://heosphorus.itch.io/blink)). Heavy eyes: half-blinks that each open a little less. Remotion's `iris` transition gives a circular reveal ([docs](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/transitions/presentations/iris)).
- **Breathing.** Bob camera Y and pitch on a sine wave, about 0.25 Hz when calm ([Live2D](https://community.live2d.com/discussion/comment/915)). In fear, 2–3 quick breaths and then slowing ([UAL notes](https://advancedandexperimentalanimationunit.myblog.arts.ac.uk/2022/06/10/week-8/)).
  → use in film: your breath slows **on screen** when you take his hand.
- **Shake.** Use seeded noise (`@remotion/noise`, [docs](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/noise)), never `Math.random()`. Spike it on impacts and let it decay.
- **Looking up.** A low angle with a 60–70° FOV, his head cropped at the top as he enters, then he kneels to your eye-line. Low angles read as awe as easily as threat ([Videomaker](https://www.videomaker.com/how-to/shooting/composition/what-is-the-low-angle-shot/), [Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/low-angle-shot)).
- **Focus and lens.** `@react-three/postprocessing` provides `DepthOfField` with a `target` you can rack between hand and face, plus Vignette, ChromaticAberration (fear) and **ShockWave** (TUNG rings) ([docs](https://react-postprocessing.docs.pmnd.rs/effects/depth-of-field)). In *The Diving Bell and the Butterfly*, actors look straight into the lens ([analysis](https://colorculture.org/the-diving-bell-and-the-butterfly-cinematography-analysis/)).
  → use in film: Sahur looks into the lens at the fire and at the wind-up.
- **Hands.** The cream mitten-hands enter from the bottom corners and lag the camera slightly, so they feel attached to a body.
- **Painted memories.** Split each painted background into 3–5 depth layers moving at different speeds, multiplane-style ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplane_camera)).
- **R3F in Remotion.**
  - Drive animation from `useCurrentFrame()` in `<ThreeCanvas>`.
  - Give `<Sequence layout="none">` to any sequence inside the canvas.
  - Render with the `angle` GL backend ([Remotion three](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/three)).
  - Motion blur: `<CameraMotionBlur>` ([docs](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/motion-blur)).
  - Our inference: postprocessing Noise and Glitch use internal time and randomness, so drive them from the frame or they'll flicker between renders.

## 5. Sound design for a wordless film

- **Tinnitus.** Low-pass the ambience and add a steady sine at about **3 kHz** (Children of Men sits around 3.9 and 5.1 kHz). **Let it carry across the cut** ([Cine-Files](https://www.thecine-files.com/the-tinnitus-trope/)). It's point-of-audition sound: we hear through the character's ears ([sonicfield](https://sonicfield.org/wiki/point-of-audition)).
  → use in film: the ring carries from the white-out into the bedroom and fades under room tone. The TUNG comes only after full quiet.
- **Underwater muffle.** Low-pass at about 400–800 Hz with the heartbeat dry on top. Use it for waking in the blue and for the pop.
- **Heartbeat.** The spacing between beats carries the emotion, and one isolated thump with silence around it hits hardest ([Morphic](https://morphic.com/resources/sounds/heartbeat-sound-effect)).
- **Silence before impact.** Cut everything (score, sirens, hum) when his hand touches your shoulder. The two wind-up TUNGs play almost dry. Then the pop.
- **Shepard tone.** An illusion of endless rising, as in *Dunkirk* ([Kottke](https://kottke.org/18/05/hans-zimmers-clever-use-of-the-shepard-scale-in-dunkirk)).
  → use in film: the Bubble's "number go up". Stopping it is the pop.
- **Sirens and blue hum.** Distant sirens with a Doppler bend, heard through walls. Mains hum plus a thin whine for the phone-blue room. **The hum's absence at wake-up says "real world".**
- **Instruments as characters.** Sahur = dry wood (kentongan, woodblock, marimba). You = breath. The Bubble = glass and pings. At the fire, his knock glitches into a digital click for one frame.
- **Sound-only ending.** An unseen source keeps the viewer imagining (acousmatic sound, Chion) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acousmatic_sound)). Hold the empty frame. One distant TUNG plays off-screen with a long tail. Never show its source.

## 6. Rights and sensitivity

**Keep it warm toward sahur**
- Indonesians embraced the meme, and police in Jombang even used it in a public campaign ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tung_Tung_Tung_Sahur)). I found no record of clerical objection.
- **Don't:**
  - use adhan or takbir audio, Qur'anic script, mosques or prayer
  - joke about fasting
  - have him raise the bat *at you* (in the bedroom it rests on his shoulder)
  - sample real patrol-sahur recordings
- **Do:**
  - frame the knock as care
  - optionally show a lock-screen clock around 03:00, sahur time
  - let the final TUNG maybe be a real neighbourhood patrol passing, so the tradition outlives the meme
- Kentongan codes vary by region: in parts of Central Java, *repeated* single beats once meant murder ([Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/life/2019/12/22/rhythmic-beats-of-kentongan-live-in-digital-era)). One isolated knock is fine, but avoid a looping 1-1-1 alarm.

**Rights (not legal advice)**
- French agency **Mementum Lab** says it represents Noxa and claims copyright and trademark rights. Spyder Games (Roblox *Steal a Brainrot*) is suing over whether the AI-made character is protectable. A hearing was set for 23 Sep 2026, and I found no outcome as of 13 Oct 2026. Mementum says it welcomes meme and social use but objects to big commercial use ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/the-battle-over-tung-tung-tung-sahur-is-testing-the-limits-of-copyright-and-trademark-law/), [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/tung-tung-sahur-is-at-the-center-of-a-bizarre-federal-custody-battle-over-brainrot-characters-3381733/)).
  → use in film: keep it non-commercial, use our own design, and credit "Tung Tung Tung Sahur created by Noxa (@noxaasht)" in the description.
- **Don't use** the original TikTok voice-over, the "popped the AI bubble" original sound, or phonk remixes. Content ID will catch them. Synthesise our own knocks.
- **Check where `TUNG TUNG TUNG SAHUUUUUUR CHOIR (1).mp3` came from.** If it's a downloaded fan remix, it carries the same risk. Keep a synthesised fallback.

## 7. Top 10 ideas for the film (ranked)

1. **The meme's line as the ending:** after the white-out, subtitle `we did it.`, the POV turns, then `…sahur?` over empty space.
2. **One distant, unseen TUNG over an ordinary morning:** him, or a real patrol? Leave it open.
3. **The Paperman rule:** amber is the only warmth and always comes from Sahur. The end card brings it back.
4. **A Shepard riser under the Bubble**, dead silence at his hand on your shoulder, two dry TUNGs, the pop, then 3 kHz tinnitus carried across the cut.
5. **Three knocks = the lore's three calls, flipped:** he came to save you, not punish you.
6. **A night bus-stop shot** framed low like Totoro: the original meme image, made warm.
7. **A Bubble that reads instantly:** iridescent gloss, near-correct hype text, an almost-right face, and faceless nodes passing one coin in a loop.
8. **Sahur's voice is wood** and fades as he weakens. At the fire, one knock glitches into a digital click.
9. **The POV kit:** asymmetric blinks, wake-up blur, breath that slows when you take his hand, he kneels to your eye-line, and he looks into the lens.
10. **The "people gave it a soul" ethos:** hand-touched wood grain against slop gloss, and credit Noxa.
