# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Players opening a single-file browser game for a short, readable action experience with story interludes. The game should be legible and playable for a broad age range, including players who need larger text and clearer UI contrast.

## Product Purpose

Sahur Protocol is a retro 2D roguelike about surviving rooms, choosing relics, and defeating The AI Bubble with Sahur as a companion. Success means the player can understand the story, controls, combat state, and transitions without confusion or overlapping audio.

## Brand Personality

Strange, urgent, heartfelt. The tone blends pixel horror, internet satire, and sincere sacrifice without becoming visually muddy or hard to read.

## Anti-references

Avoid tiny pixel text, static cutscene panels, overlapping music layers, unclear state changes between levels and story, and menus that feel like placeholders.

## Design Principles

- Make story moments feel alive with motion, sound, and pacing.
- Keep game state explicit: menu, cutscene, level, boss, finale, and end screens should each own input and audio.
- Prioritize readability over retro authenticity when the two conflict.
- Preserve the one-file, no-build portability of the game.
- Make difficulty climb toward the final Bubble fight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use larger text, stronger contrast, clear labels, reduced-motion fallbacks, and audio muting that applies consistently across gameplay and cutscenes.
