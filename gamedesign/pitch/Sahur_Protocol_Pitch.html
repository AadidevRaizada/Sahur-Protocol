YOUR COMPANION

Meet Sahur.
a.k.a. Triple T

  • Fights beside you. His ground-slam TUNG hits everything near him, right on the beat.
  • He can fall. Bosses snipe at him. If he goes down, you stand by him and pull him up.
  • He talks back. Deadpan one-liners mid-fight: "You swing like you scroll. Tiny."

Inspired by the Indonesian kentongan tradition of knocking on wood to wake people before dawn, made with respect.

~45 s · INTERACTIVE: click Sahur to make him swing and say a random line from the game. SAY: "This is Sahur. He's a wooden log with a baseball bat. The internet calls him Triple T, because he always knocks three times." → "He fights next to you." → "In boss fights the bosses aim at HIM, and if he falls you have to go and stand by him. Players protect him, and that's how they start caring." → "And he's funny." Mention the kentongan tradition: knocking on wood to wake people for the pre-dawn meal in Indonesia.
